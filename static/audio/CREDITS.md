# Engine audio credits

These audio files are adapted from actual Mazda MX-5 recordings shared on Freesound. They are hosted with this portfolio; driving does not stream audio from Freesound.

## Idle, ignition and engine stop

**“Miata Start and Stop.mp3” — TurboTheSergal** (June 22, 2020)

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/TurboTheSergal/sounds/523351/
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International — https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Downloaded audio: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/523/523351_11603686-hq.mp3
- Adapted files: `mx5-idle.wav`, `mx5-start.wav`, `mx5-stop.wav`.
- Changes: cropped at 7.00–9.20 s, 4.45–5.55 s and 9.60–10.25 s respectively; converted to mono 22,050 Hz / 16-bit PCM WAV, filtered below 28 Hz and above 4,200 Hz, level adjusted; idle loop ends crossfaded, one-shot edges faded. The original engine shutdown is used as the stall sound. These adapted files remain available under CC BY 4.0.

## Low and high rev loops

**“car revving.aif” — Walter_Odington** (May 2, 2006)

- Source: https://freesound.org/people/Walter_Odington/sounds/18620/
- License: Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal — https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
- Downloaded audio: https://cdn.freesound.org/previews/18/18620_48671-hq.mp3
- Adapted files: `mx5-low.wav`, `mx5-high.wav`.
- Changes: cropped at 3.90–5.20 s and 7.00–7.47 s respectively; converted to mono 22,050 Hz / 16-bit PCM WAV, filtered below 28 Hz and above 4,200 Hz, level adjusted, and loop ends crossfaded.

The rev recording is an Eunos Roadster with an HKS exhaust, as described by its author. Playback pitch, filtering and blending adapt these recordings to the game's engine speed and throttle. This is an approximation of an MX-5 engine, not a measured stock NB2 recording set. The authors do not endorse this portfolio.
